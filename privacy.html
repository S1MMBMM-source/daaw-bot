Daaw Bot - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

1. Introduction

This policy explains how Daaw Bot processes information when users interact with it on Discord.

2. Information Processed

Depending on the commands used, the bot may process Discord user IDs, server IDs, usernames, display names and information provided in commands.

3. How Information Is Used

Information is used to execute commands, provide moderation features, respond to requests and maintain the bot.

4. Data Storage

Information is stored only if necessary for the bot's functionality. The developer should document what information is stored, where it is stored and when it is deleted.

5. Sharing

Daaw Bot does not sell personal information. Discord and necessary service providers may process information as part of operating the bot.

6. Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect information.

7. Data Requests

For questions or requests concerning personal information, contact the developer.
